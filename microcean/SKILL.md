## One-paragraph summary
Microcean is a concept for a single planetary medium ("Ocean") where software exists as tiny, content-addressed, tested, resource-annotated **fragments** ("plankton"). A swarm of narrow agents assembles programs from fragments by evolutionary search under an objective Φ(S)=α·Err+β·cost+γ·incomp with an MDL "zero excess" pressure. Discovery runs on Kademlia DHT, updates on gossip, the registry on CRDTs, trust on executable tests + citation-graph reputation + economic stake. Organizations/states keep closed **lagoons** with selective bridges. The site is published as defensive prior art under CC0.
